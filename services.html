A LITTLE CLARITY BEFORE YOU VISIT

Good questions. Clear answers.

Can I ask for a different ingredient?

Talk to the café about your preferences. Substitutions depend on the recipe and ingredient availability.

Where can I find allergen information?

Explore each product’s ingredient notes and the Nutrition Info page. Discuss allergies directly with the café before ordering, including possible shared-equipment contact.

Can I plan a group visit?

Use the Contact page to ask about your group size, preferred date and seating needs. The café will need to confirm arrangements.

Does the website accept paid orders?

The current website provides a demonstration enquiry flow. Final menu availability, prices and ordering arrangements are confirmed by the café.
